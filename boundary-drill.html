The boundary

Boundary accuracy

Scored on cold attempts only — a scenario you've never seen, or haven't seen for 14 days — most recent 10. Same-week repeats are tracked separately and never touch the band. Under 10 cold answers a boundary shows as provisional; five attempts cannot separate a 60% boundary from an 85% one.

Under 60% — not held 60–79% — shaky 80%+ — holding

Miss a scenario and the drill serves three more on that same boundary before moving on — the three-novel-scenarios rule, automated. Progress saves in this browser only.

Recall the expansion

Hint used — the best grade available is Partly. Cued recall is easier than what the exam asks for, so it shouldn't earn the same promotion.

Partly = you named the right thing but missed what separates it from its neighbours. Couldn't place it at all? That's missed. Partly drops the card a box — it never advances one.

Card progress

Five boxes. Knew it moves a card up, partly drops it one, missed sends it back to box 1. Higher boxes come round less often — box 5 is once a fortnight.

Score yourself honestly. If you had to think for more than about three seconds, that's a miss. Self-rated recall is where people flatter themselves, and a card you half-knew is a card you won't have under exam pressure.

Acronyms are the one place definition cards are the right tool — there's no judgement involved, and not knowing what a term expands to means the question is lost before your reasoning starts. Ten minutes a day. It is a prerequisite, not where your points are.
